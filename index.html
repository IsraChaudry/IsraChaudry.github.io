// About me

Building at the intersection of AI & Security

I'm a 3rd-year BS Cybersecurity student at GIKI, Pakistan, with a deep focus on applied AI and defensive security. I build systems that are both intelligent and hardened — from fine-tuned NLP models to blockchain-backed identity platforms.

Currently leading development of CIVIC EYE — my Final Year Project — a smart surveillance platform integrating YOLOv8 computer vision with real-time threat detection for public safety infrastructure.
